# Austria · AT

Sources verified November 25, 2026.

## Government mailbox

Mein Postkorb allows companies to automatically pick up inboxes through a web service. The connection activates the authorized recipient, then the USP administrator sets up the certificates. The official guide describes the test environment. [Automatic receipt](https://www.usp.gv.at/en/mein-postkorb/einstellungen/automatische-abholung.html) · [Technical guidance](https://www.usp.gv.at/dam/jcr%3A0909b669-4372-438f-b3fd-c42a37ffc5f4/Mein_Postkorb_AutomatischeAbholung_HowTo.pdf)

## Use case and limitations

Bell can supplement the official channel with local archives and routing, determine what action is legal confirmation of admission and what authority is needed to read attachments, universal filing and public service status are not confirmed here, and the publicity of the full platform source is not established.

The GovAPI Hub plug-in is not yet available.
